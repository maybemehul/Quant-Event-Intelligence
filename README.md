# Smart Market Watch

### Know what changed. Know what matters.

Smart Market Watch is an evidence-driven market watchlist built around a simple question:

> What actually changed enough for me to care?

Traditional watchlists show prices and percentage movements, but leave users to repeatedly check every stock themselves.

Smart Market Watch analyzes market activity, identifies statistically meaningful changes, and surfaces the stocks that deserve attention.

## Live Demo

https://smart-market-watch.vercel.app

## What It Does

- Add and manage stocks in a persistent watchlist
- Track current market prices and trading activity
- Compare recent movement against historical behaviour
- Detect unusual price and volume activity
- Assign an attention priority to meaningful changes
- Show the statistical evidence behind each alert
- Explore historical price movement
- Generate evidence-grounded AI analysis

The goal is not to make users monitor markets constantly.

It is to help them return later and quickly understand:

> What changed since I last checked?
